# Four-Year Profit Optimization Challenge V4

## Selection rule

- Year 1: exactly two activities
- Year 2: exactly two activities
- Year 3: exactly two activities
- Year 4: at least one activity
- The same continuous-improvement activity may be deployed in multiple years.
- The first deployment receives 100% of the stated yearly effect, the second receives 40%, and the third receives 15%, representing diminishing marginal returns.
- A team must finish Year 4 with zero budget to receive an eligible final leaderboard score.

## Baseline

- Productivity: 80 tiles/day
- Quality: 75%
- Manufacturing cost: 30/tile
- Selling price: 50/tile
- Budget: 100 points

## GitHub deployment

Upload all files to the repository root and deploy `app.py` in Streamlit Community Cloud.
